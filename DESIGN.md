# Overview
The reference site's confident, service-led hierarchy is translated into a PrimeTech world: rounded action controls, white space, blue focal fields and real worksite photography. No synthetic project pictures or fabricated proof.
# Colors
PrimeTech navy #163f73 directs primary actions and contact. Deep navy #0d2b51 anchors the statement and CTA areas; paper #fbfcfd provides breathing room; sky blue #548fc7 supplies small wayfinding accents.
# Typography
Manrope is the compact, assertive display face. DM Sans handles body copy and navigation. Large headings use tight tracking and short line lengths.
# Layout
The page opens with a strong two-column hero, then moves from project principles to service cards, work samples and a substantial contact panel. Mobile keeps the hero photograph and converts the navigation into a keyboard-accessible menu.
# Elevation & Depth
Cards have restrained soft shadow and rounded corners; photography remains the primary source of depth.
# Shapes
Cards use gentle 10–14px corners. Buttons and small action controls are pill-shaped.
# Components
Native service disclosures, navigation anchors, phone and email links, external Instagram project links.
# Do's and Don'ts
Use actual company photographs. Keep contact details sourced. Do not invent service areas, testimonials, guarantees or metrics.
